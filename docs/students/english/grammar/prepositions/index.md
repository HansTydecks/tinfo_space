# Prepositions

## Goals
- understand what a preposition is
- know the eight types of prepositions
- practise them with the Silent Teacher until you don’t have to think about them

## What is a preposition?

A **preposition** is a small word that **connects** a word with the rest of the sentence.
It shows *when*, *where*, *where to*, *how*, *who*, *how much*, *where from* or *whose*.

*Eine **Präposition** (Verhältniswort) ist ein kleines Wort wie* in, on, at, with, from.
*Sie zeigt, in welchem Verhältnis zwei Dinge stehen – zeitlich, räumlich oder auf andere Weise.*

> The cat is **under** the table. – We meet **at** 7 o’clock. – I go to school **by** bus.

A preposition usually stands **before** a noun or a pronoun: *in **the box***, *with **me***, *from **Germany***.

::: tip Why are prepositions so hard?
In German and English, prepositions often don’t match: *am Montag* → **on** Monday,
*um 7 Uhr* → **at** 7 o’clock, *im Bus* → **on** the bus. So don’t translate word by word –
learn prepositions together with the words they go with, and **practise a lot**.
:::

## The eight types of prepositions

| Type | Question | Examples | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⏰ Time | When? How long? | at 7 o’clock, on Monday, in July, for two hours | [Prepositions of time](./time) |
| 📦 Place | Where? | in, on, under, next to, behind, between | [Prepositions of place](./place) |
| ➡️ Movement | Where to? | into, out of, up, down, across, through | [Prepositions of movement](./movement) |
| 🚲 Manner | How? | by bus, on foot, in English, with a smile, like a bird | [Prepositions of manner](./manner) |
| ✍️ Agent & instrument | Who did it? With what? | written **by** Shakespeare, cut **with** scissors | [Agent & instrument](./agent-instrument) |
| ⚖️ Measure | How much? | a cup **of** tea, up **by** 10 %, 50 km **per** hour | [Prepositions of measure](./measure) |
| 🌍 Source | Where from? | **from** Germany, made **of** wood, a song **by** Adele | [Prepositions of source](./source) |
| 🔑 Possession | Whose? What does it have? | the end **of** the film, a girl **with** long hair, belongs **to** me | [Prepositions of possession](./possession) |

::: info Some prepositions have many jobs
The same word can belong to different types:
- **by**: *by 5 o’clock* (time), *by the window* (place), *by bus* (manner), *written by Shakespeare* (agent)
- **in**: *in July* (time), *in the box* (place), *in English* (manner)

So always look at the **whole sentence**.
:::

## How to learn prepositions

1. **Start with time and place.** *at, on, in* are the most important prepositions of all.
2. **Learn chunks, not single words:** not just *on*, but *on Monday*, *on the bus*, *on TV*.
3. **Practise with the Silent Teacher.** You see a sentence, you choose the preposition. At first you
   guess – after a few mistakes you see the rule. Every chapter has its own Silent Teacher.
4. **Come back the next day.** Short practice every day works better than one long session.
