# Prepositions of measure

## Goals
- talk about **amounts**: *a cup **of** tea, a kilo **of** apples*
- use **by** and **per** for differences and rates
- use *at, out of, about, up to, over, under* with numbers

Prepositions of measure answer the question **How much? How many? How fast?** – *Wie viel?*

## of – amounts

| | |
|---|---|
| a cup **of** tea | a glass **of** water |
| a kilo **of** apples | a bottle **of** milk |
| a piece **of** cake | a slice **of** pizza |
| a bar **of** chocolate | a pair **of** socks |
| a lot **of** homework | half **of** the class |

::: danger Typical mistake
German has no preposition here (*eine Tasse Tee*) – English needs **of**: a cup **of** tea (~~a cup tea~~).
:::

## by and per

| | meaning | examples |
|---|---|---|
| **by** | difference | Prices went up **by** 10 %. – We won **by** two goals. |
| **by** | unit | They are paid **by** the hour. – Apples are sold **by** the kilo. |
| **by** | maths & size | 4 multiplied **by** 3 – a room 5 metres **by** 4 metres |
| **per** | *pro* | 10 euros **per** person – 50 km **per** hour – three times **per** day |

## at, out of, about, up to, over, under

| | Deutsch | example |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | bei (speed, temperature, price) | The train travels **at** 200 km/h. – Water boils **at** 100 °C. |
| **out of** | von | Nine **out of** ten people like chocolate. – I got 18 **out of** 20. |
| **about** | ungefähr | It’s **about** 20 km from here. |
| **up to** | bis zu (maximum) | The lift carries **up to** eight people. |
| **over** | über (more than) | This film is only for people **over** 18. |
| **under** | unter (less than) | Children **under** six travel for free. |

## Practise

Levels: **a cup of tea** · **by and per** · **at, out of, about, up to …**
