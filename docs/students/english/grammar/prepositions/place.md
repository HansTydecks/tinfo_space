# Prepositions of place

## Goals
- say **where** something is: *in, on, under, above, next to, behind, in front of, between*
- choose between **at**, **in** and **on** for places
- use *near, opposite, among*

Prepositions of place answer the question **Where?** – *Wo ist etwas?*

| English | Deutsch | example |
|---|---|---|
| **in** | in | The cat is **in** the box. |
| **on** | auf | The phone is **on** the table. |
| **under** | unter | The dog is sleeping **under** the table. |
| **above** | über (ohne Berührung) | The lamp is **above** the table. |
| **next to** | neben | I sit **next to** my best friend. |
| **behind** | hinter | The garden is **behind** the house. |
| **in front of** | vor | The bus stop is **in front of** the school. |
| **between** | zwischen | B is **between** A and C. |

::: warning on or above?
**on** = it **touches** the top: *The book is **on** the table.*
**above** = it is higher, but does **not** touch: *The lamp is **above** the table.*
:::

::: danger in front of – not „before"
German *vor* (place) = **in front of**: *I’m waiting **in front of** the cinema.*
*before* is for **time**: *before dinner*.
:::

## at, in or on?

| | used for | examples |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | a **point** or a place where you **do** something | **at** home, **at** school, **at** work, **at** the bus stop, **at** the station, **at** the door, **at** a party, **at** the end of the street |
| **in** | **inside** a space, a town, a country | **in** the kitchen, **in** my bag, **in** bed, **in** the garden, **in** London, **in** Germany, **in** the sky, **in** a book |
| **on** | a **surface** or a **line** | **on** the wall, **on** the table, **on** the floor, **on** the second floor, **on** the left, **on** page 12, **on** the map, **on** TV, **on** the internet |

::: tip Memory trick
**at** = a dot on the map 📍 · **in** = inside a box 📦 · **on** = on top of a surface 📄
:::

Some fixed phrases you simply have to learn:
- **at** home, **at** school, **at** work – but **in** bed, **in** hospital
- **in** the car / **in** a taxi – but **on** the bus / **on** the train / **on** the plane

## near, opposite, among

| | Deutsch | example |
|---|---|---|
| **near** | in der Nähe von | We live **near** the sea. |
| **opposite** | gegenüber | The bank is **opposite** the post office. |
| **between** | zwischen (two things) | I sat **between** my mum and my dad. |
| **among** | (mitten) unter (many things) | I found my ring **among** the leaves. |
