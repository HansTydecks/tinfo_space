# Prepositions of agent & instrument

## Goals
- use **by** for the person (or thing) that does something – especially in the passive
- use **with** for the tool you use
- use **by + -ing** to say *how* you do something

*Wer hat es getan?* → **by** · *Womit?* → **with**

## by – the agent (who did it?)

The **agent** is the person or thing that does the action. In **passive** sentences we use **by**:

- *Harry Potter* was written **by** J. K. Rowling. (*von J. K. Rowling*)
- The mouse was caught **by** the cat.
- The town was destroyed **by** a fire.
- The thief was arrested **by** the police.

::: danger Typical mistake: „von"
German *von* is not always *from*!
*Das Buch wurde von J. K. Rowling geschrieben.* → written **by** J. K. Rowling (~~from~~, ~~of~~)
:::

## with – the instrument (with what?)

The **instrument** is the **tool** or thing you use:

- She cut the paper **with** scissors. (*mit einer Schere*)
- He opened the door **with** a key.
- We eat soup **with** a spoon.
- Don’t eat **with** your fingers!

## by or with?

| question | preposition | example |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** did it? | **by** | The letter was written **by** my best friend. |
| **With what** did they do it? | **with** | The letter was written **with** a pencil. |
| **How** do you do it? (+ -ing) | **by** | You start the game **by pressing** the green button. |

::: tip Test question
Can you ask **“Who?”** → **by**. Can you ask **“With what?”** → **with**.
:::

Levels: **by – who did it?** · **with – what tool?** · **by or with?**
