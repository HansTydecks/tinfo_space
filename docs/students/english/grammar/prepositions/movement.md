# Prepositions of movement

## Goals
- say **where to** someone or something moves
- tell the difference between *in* (place) and *into* (movement)
- use *go to school*, *go home*, *get on the bus*, *arrive at / in* correctly

Prepositions of movement answer the question **Where to?** – *Wohin bewegt sich etwas?*

## into, out of, onto, off

<PrepositionPictures :items="[
  ['m-into', 'into the box'],
  ['m-out-of', 'out of the box'],
  ['m-onto', 'onto the box'],
  ['m-off', 'off the box'],
]" />

| English | Deutsch | example |
|---|---|---|
| **into** | in … hinein | The cat jumps **into** the box. |
| **out of** | aus … heraus | Get **out of** the water! |
| **onto** | auf … hinauf | The cat jumps **onto** the table. |
| **off** | von … herunter | He fell **off** his bike. |

::: tip in or into?
**in** = the place (no movement): *The cat is **in** the box.*
**into** = the movement from outside to inside: *The cat jumps **into** the box.*
:::

| English | Deutsch | example |
|---|---|---|
| **up** | hinauf | We walked **up** the hill. |
| **down** | hinunter | The ball rolled **down** the stairs. |
| **over** | über … hinweg | The horse jumps **over** the fence. |
| **under** | unter … hindurch | The boat goes **under** the bridge. |
| **through** | durch | The train goes **through** the tunnel. |
| **around** | um … herum | We ran **around** the lake. |

## across, along, past, to, towards

<PrepositionPictures :items="[
  ['m-across', 'across the river', '🐶'],
  ['m-along', 'along the river', '🦆'],
  ['m-past', 'past the house', '🚶'],
  ['m-to', 'to the house', '🚶'],
  ['m-towards', 'towards the house', '🚶'],
]" />

| English | Deutsch | example |
|---|---|---|
| **across** | über (auf die andere Seite) | Look left and right before you walk **across** the street. |
| **along** | entlang | We walked **along** the beach. |
| **past** | an … vorbei | The bus drove **past** our school. |
| **to** | zu, nach (you arrive) | I go **to** school by bus. |
| **towards** | in Richtung, auf … zu | A big dog ran **towards** me! |

## go, get, arrive

| ✅ right | ❌ wrong | rule |
|---|---|---|
| go **to** school, go **to** bed, go **to** London | | go **to** + place |
| go **home**, come **home**, get **home** | ~~go to home~~ | **no** preposition before *home* |
| get **on** / **off** the bus, the train, the plane, your bike | | big vehicles you can stand in, bikes |
| get **into** / **out of** the car, a taxi | | small vehicles |
| arrive **at** the station, **at** school, **at** the airport | ~~arrive to the station~~ | arrive **at** + building / small place |
| arrive **in** London, **in** Germany | ~~arrive to London~~ | arrive **in** + town / country |

::: danger Typical mistake
*Ich gehe nach Hause.* → I go **home**. (~~I go to home.~~)
:::
